## Brief

A dashboard for a small engineering team's incidents. It shows what is on fire right now, who is on call, the last thirty days of incidents and how long each took to resolve. The on-call person opens it at 3 am, so the open incident has to be impossible to miss.

## Answers

- who: A five-person engineering team
- where: Desktop, sometimes a phone at night
- feel: Clear and steady under pressure
- system: None, start fresh
