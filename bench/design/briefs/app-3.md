## Brief

The first three screens of onboarding for a habit tracking app: a welcome, picking up to three habits, and choosing a reminder time. Someone should be done in under a minute and feel like they already started.

## Answers

- who: People who have tried habit apps before and given up
- where: Phone
- feel: Encouraging without being cute
- system: None, start fresh
